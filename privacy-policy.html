Privacy Policy

At AIAHM (AviIndia Institute Of Aviation and Hospitality Management), we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use and safeguard the information you provide when visiting our website or submitting enquiry forms.


1. Information We Collect
When you fill out our enquiry form, we may collect:

  • Full Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • Interested Course
  • Qualification
  • Any additional message you submit

We may also collect limited technical information such as IP address and browser type for website analytics and advertising purposes.


2. How We Use Your Information
We use your information to:

  • Respond to your enquiries
  • Provide course details and admission information
  • Contact you via phone, email or WhatsApp regarding our programs
  • Improve our services and website experience
  • Run advertising campaigns and measure performance
  • We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Protection

  • We take appropriate security measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, misuse or disclosure.
  • Your information is accessible only to authorized staff members for official purposes.

4. Cookies & Advertising

  • Our website may use cookies and third-party tools such as Meta (Facebook) Pixel or Google Analytics to improve user experience and measure advertising performance.
  • These tools may collect anonymized data to help us understand user behavior and improve our marketing efforts.

5. Third-Party Links

  • Our website may contain links to third-party platforms such as Facebook, Instagram or WhatsApp. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those platforms.

6. Your Consent

  • By using our website and submitting your details, you consent to the collection and use of your information as described in this Privacy Policy.

7. Updates to This Policy

  • We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
