# ✈️ AIAHM – Aviation & Hospitality Institute Website

Official website for **AviIndia Institute Of Aviation & Hospitality Management (AIAHM)**.  
This project is a modern, responsive, and user-friendly website built to showcase the institute’s courses, admissions, placements, and contact information.

---

## 🌐 Live Website
https://www.aiahm.in/

---

## 📌 Project Overview

AiAHM is a professional training institute offering aviation, hospitality, ground services, and customer service programs.  
This website is designed to:

- Build trust and credibility
- Generate student enquiries
- Showcase courses & placements
- Provide clear admission guidance

---

## 🧩 Pages Included

- **Home** – Hero section, highlights, CTA  
- **About Us** – Institute profile, mission, vision  
- **Courses** – Aviation & hospitality programs  
- **Admission** – Process & eligibility  
- **Placement** – Partners & success stories  
- **Media Gallery** – Campus & events  
- **Careers** – Faculty & staff openings  
- **Our Team** – Management & trainers  
- **Contact Us** – Location, phone, email  
- **Enquiry** – Dedicated lead form
